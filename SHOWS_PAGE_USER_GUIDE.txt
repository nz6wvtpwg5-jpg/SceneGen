                          User Guide (Plain English)
================================================================================

📍 WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
================================================================================

The /shows page is your command center for managing all your concert dates
and gigs. Think of it like a tour calendar meets a spreadsheet, but way more
user-friendly.


================================================================================
✅ WHAT CURRENTLY WORKS
================================================================================

1. VIEW ALL YOUR SHOWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   • See a list of all your upcoming concerts/gigs
   • Shows appear in a clean table on the left side
   • Each show displays:
     - Venue name
     - Date and time
     - Location (city, state)
     - Status badge (Confirmed, Pending, Draft, Cancelled)
     - Door time and show time


2. QUICK PREVIEW ON THE RIGHT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   • The right side shows a compact, scrollable list of your upcoming shows
   • Perfect for quick scanning without scrolling through the full table
   • Shows the most important info at a glance
   • Stays visible as you scroll (sticky rail)


3. FILTER YOUR SHOWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   • BY STATUS: See only Confirmed, Pending, Draft, or Cancelled shows
   • BY TOUR: Group shows by which tour they're part of
   • SEARCH: Type to find specific venues or cities
   • Shows update instantly as you filter


4. GOOGLE CALENDAR INTEGRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   • "Add to Calendar" button on each show
     - Syncs the show to your Google Calendar
     - Creates a proper event with venue, time, and location
     - Button changes to "Synced ✓" after adding (but there's currently
       a bug where this doesn't persist - we're working on it)

   • "Connect Google Calendar" button at the top
     - Links your Google account once
     - After that, all shows can be synced with one click


5. SELECT MULTIPLE SHOWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   • Checkboxes on each show row
   • "Select All" checkbox in the header
   • Once selected, you get bulk actions (like "Add to Tour")


6. CALENDAR VIEW TOGGLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   • Switch between List View (table) and Calendar View (monthly grid)
   • Calendar view shows your shows as events on a visual calendar
   • Navigate between months
   • See which days have shows at a glance


7. CREATE NEW SHOWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   • Big blue "Add Show" button at the top right
   • Opens a modal form to enter show details:
     - Venue information
     - Date and time
     - Deal details (guarantee, door split, etc.)
     - Contact information


8. INDIVIDUAL SHOW ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Each show has action buttons:
   • EDIT - Modify show details
   • ADD TO CALENDAR - Sync to Google Calendar
   • CANCEL SHOW - Mark the show as cancelled

🎸 FEATURES IN DETAIL
================================================================================

STATUS BADGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Shows have color-coded status badges:
   • GREEN = Confirmed (locked in, ready to go)
   • YELLOW = Pending (waiting for confirmation)
   • BLUE = Draft (still working on details)
   • RED = Cancelled (show won't happen)


FILTERING OPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   • All (2) - See all shows regardless of status
   • Confirmed (2) - Only shows that are locked in
   • Pending (0) - Shows awaiting confirmation
   • Draft (0) - Shows still being planned
   • Cancelled (0) - Shows that won't happen

   • All Tours - See shows from all tours
   • Standalone - Shows not part of any tour
   • [Tour Name] - Shows from a specific tour


BULK ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Select multiple shows with checkboxes, then:
   • Add to Tour - Group multiple shows into a tour
   • Remove from Tour - Take shows out of a tour
   • Clear Selection - Unselect all shows


CALENDAR VIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   • Monthly grid layout (like Google Calendar)
   • Shows appear on their scheduled dates
   • Click on a show to see details
   • Navigate between months with arrow buttons
   • Today button to jump to current month
