WHAT IS SCENEGEN?
================================================================================

SceneGen is a platform that connects musicians, promoters, and venues to make
booking live shows easier. Think of it as a combination of LinkedIn for the
music industry and a tour management tool.

WHO IS IT FOR?

- Musicians/Bands looking for gigs
- Promoters organizing concerts and events
- Venues hosting live music
- Anyone involved in booking live shows

WHAT PROBLEM DOES IT SOLVE?

Currently, booking shows involves endless emails, phone calls, and scattered
spreadsheets. SceneGen brings everything into one place:
- Find and connect with the right people
- Manage your shows and tours
- Communicate directly with other industry professionals
- Keep track of all your bookings in one organized system

================================================================================
YOUR EXPERIENCE: GETTING STARTED
================================================================================

SIGNING UP & LOGGING IN

When you first visit SceneGen, you'll see a welcoming landing page. To get
started, simply click "Sign In" and choose how you want to log in:

- Sign in with GitHub
- Sign in with Google
- (More options coming soon)

No need to remember another password - just use an account you already have!

FIRST-TIME SETUP

After signing in, you'll be asked to:
1. Choose your role (Artist, Promoter, or Venue)
2. Complete your profile with basic information
3. Add details relevant to your role (genres, location, etc.)

That's it! You're ready to start using SceneGen.

================================================================================
MAIN FEATURES & HOW TO USE THEM
================================================================================

1. YOUR DASHBOARD (Home Base)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

When you log in, you'll land on your dashboard - think of this as your
command center.

WHAT YOU'LL SEE:
- Welcome message with your name
- Quick overview of your upcoming shows
- Recent messages
- Pending connection requests
- Important notifications

WHAT YOU CAN DO:
- Click on any show to see details
- Respond to messages
- Accept or decline connection requests
- Jump to any section using the sidebar

USER EXPERIENCE:
The dashboard is clean and uncluttered. You'll immediately see what needs
your attention (like new messages or requests) and get a snapshot of your
upcoming schedule. Everything is just one click away.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2. YOUR PROFILE (Your Musical Identity)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Your profile is your digital business card in the music industry.

WHAT'S INCLUDED:
- Profile photo and cover image
- Your bio (tell your story!)
- Music genres you play or work with
- Location (city, state, country)
- Social media links (Spotify, Instagram, Facebook, website)
- For bands: List of band members
- Your upcoming shows
- Contact button for others to reach you

HOW TO UPDATE YOUR PROFILE:
1. Go to your profile page
2. Click "Edit Profile"
3. Update any information
4. Upload images if you want
5. Click "Save Changes"

USER EXPERIENCE:
Editing your profile feels intuitive - it's like filling out a form on any
social media site. You'll see a live preview as you make changes. When you
save, you'll get a confirmation message letting you know it worked. If
something goes wrong, you'll see a helpful error message explaining what
needs to be fixed.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3. FINDING & CONNECTING WITH PEOPLE (Networking Made Easy)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The Contacts section helps you find and connect with other music industry
professionals.

HOW TO SEARCH FOR PEOPLE:
1. Go to "Contacts" in the sidebar
2. Use the search bar to find people by name
3. Filter by:
   - Role (Artists, Promoters, Venues)
   - Location (find people in your area)
   - Genre (find your musical match)
4. Browse the results

WHAT YOU'LL SEE FOR EACH PERSON:
- Profile photo
- Name and role
- Location
- Music genres
- A preview of their bio
- "Connect" button

HOW TO SEND A CONNECTION REQUEST:
1. Find someone you want to connect with
2. Click the "Connect" button
3. (Optional) Add a personal message
4. Click "Send Request"
5. You'll see a confirmation that your request was sent

MANAGING CONNECTION REQUESTS:
There's a "Pending Requests" tab where you can:
- See who wants to connect with you
- View their profile
- Accept or decline the request
- Each request shows when it was sent

VIEWING YOUR CONNECTIONS:
Once people accept your requests, they appear in your "Connections" list.
From here you can:
- View their full profile
- Send them a message
- See their contact information

USER EXPERIENCE:
Finding people is as easy as using any social media search. The interface
is clean with clear buttons. When you send a request, you immediately see
a green success message. If something goes wrong, you'll see a red message
explaining the issue.

If you have no connections yet, you'll see a friendly empty state message
saying "No contacts yet" with suggestions on how to find people. This makes
the platform feel welcoming, not intimidating.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4. MESSAGING (Direct Communication)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Once you're connected with someone, you can message them directly.

HOW MESSAGING WORKS:
1. Go to "Messages" or "Conversations" in the sidebar
2. You'll see a list of all your conversations
3. Click on any conversation to open it
4. Type your message in the box at the bottom
5. Press Enter or click "Send"

WHAT YOU'LL SEE:
- Left side: List of all conversations with preview of last message
- Right side: Full conversation with message history
- Unread message indicators (badges with numbers)
- Timestamps showing when messages were sent
- Profile photos next to each message

CONVERSATION LIST FEATURES:
- See who you're chatting with
- Preview of the last message
- How long ago the last message was sent
- Unread message count
- Search through conversations

USER EXPERIENCE:
The messaging feels like texting or any modern chat app. Your messages
appear on the right in blue, theirs appear on the left in gray. New
messages appear instantly. You'll see when someone is typing (coming soon).
The interface is familiar and comfortable.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5. SHOWS (Managing Your Gigs)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This is where you see all shows - both yours and ones you might be
interested in.

VIEW OPTIONS:
You can view shows in two ways:
- Table View (list format with sortable columns)
- Calendar View (visual calendar showing dates)

SHOWS TABLE:
Shows are displayed with the following information:
- Show title
- Date and time
- Venue name and location
- Artists performing
- Status (Upcoming, Completed, Cancelled)
- Actions (View details, Edit, Add to tour)

FILTERING SHOWS:
Use the filters at the top to narrow down shows:
- Search by name
- Filter by date range
- Filter by location (city, state, country)
- Filter by genre
- Filter by status
- Filter by tour (shows assigned to specific tours)

CALENDAR VIEW:
- See shows laid out on a monthly calendar
- Each show appears on its date
- Different colors for different types
- Click any show to see details
- Navigate between months easily

SELECTING MULTIPLE SHOWS (Bulk Actions):
This is a powerful feature for managing many shows at once:

1. Click the checkbox next to any shows you want to select
2. A toolbar appears at the top showing how many you selected
3. Choose an action:
   - Add to Tour (assign multiple shows to a tour)
   - Remove from Tour (take shows off a tour)
   - More options coming soon

4. Confirm your action
5. You'll see a success message showing how many shows were updated

USER EXPERIENCE:
When you first visit the Shows page, you see a loading animation (skeleton
placeholder) for a moment while data loads - this makes the page feel fast
and responsive.

If you have no shows yet, you'll see a welcoming empty state with a
calendar icon and message saying "No shows yet" with a button to create
your first show.

After performing any action (like adding shows to a tour), you'll see a
popup notification at the bottom of the screen confirming what happened.
These notifications automatically disappear after a few seconds.

The checkbox selection feels smooth - you can select one show or a hundred.
The bulk toolbar slides in nicely when you make selections.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6. TOURS (Organizing Multiple Shows)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Tours help you organize multiple shows into a single group, like a summer
tour or a regional run of shows.

WHAT YOU'LL SEE:
- List of all your tours
- Each tour shows:
  - Tour name
  - Date range (start to end)
  - Number of shows
  - Status (Planning, Active, Completed)
  - Quick actions

CREATING A NEW TOUR:
1. Click the "Create Tour" button
2. A window pops up with a form
3. Fill in:
   - Tour name (e.g., "Summer Tour 2026")
   - Description (optional details about the tour)
   - Start date
   - End date
4. Click "Create Tour"
5. You'll see a success message
6. The tour appears in your list

VIEWING TOUR DETAILS:
Click on any tour to see:
- Full tour information
- List of all shows in the tour
- Show dates, venues, and locations
- Options to edit or delete the tour

MANAGING SHOWS IN A TOUR:
From the tour details page:
- See all shows assigned to this tour in a table
- Select shows using checkboxes
- Remove shows from the tour (bulk action)
- Click any show to see its full details

EDITING A TOUR:
1. Go to the tour details page
2. Click "Edit Tour"
3. Update the information
4. Click "Save Changes"
5. See confirmation that it worked

DELETING A TOUR:
1. Click "Delete Tour" button
2. A confirmation dialog appears asking "Are you sure?"
3. Confirm the deletion
4. Tour is removed (but the shows still exist)

ADDING SHOWS TO A TOUR:
You can add shows to a tour from two places:

Option A - From Shows Page:
1. Select shows using checkboxes
2. Click "Add to Tour" in the toolbar
3. Choose which tour from a dropdown
4. Or create a new tour right there
5. Confirm
6. Success message appears

Option B - From Individual Show:
1. View a show's details
2. Click "Add to Tour"
3. Select the tour
4. Confirm

USER EXPERIENCE:
Tours make organizing complex bookings simple. The interface is clean and
focused. When you create a tour, the modal (popup window) is easy to
understand with clearly labeled fields.

If you don't have any tours yet, you'll see a friendly empty state showing
a route icon with "No tours yet" and a clear "Create Your First Tour"
button.

Loading states show skeleton placeholders, so you never see a blank screen.
All actions give you immediate feedback with popup notifications.

FEEDBACK & COMMUNICATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The platform constantly communicates with you about what's happening:

LOADING STATES:
When data is loading, you'll see:
- Animated skeleton placeholders (outlines of content)
- Spinning indicators for quick actions
- "Loading..." text for longer operations
- Progress bars for uploads (coming soon)

You'll never see a blank screen wondering if something broke.

SUCCESS MESSAGES:
When you complete an action successfully:
- Green popup notification at the bottom of the screen
- Checkmark icon
- Clear message like "Tour created successfully!"
- Automatically disappears after 4 seconds
- Or you can dismiss it immediately

Examples:
- "Show added to tour!"
- "Profile updated successfully"
- "Connection request sent"
- "Message sent"

ERROR MESSAGES:
If something goes wrong:
- Red popup notification
- X icon
- Helpful message explaining what happened
- Sometimes a "Retry" button
- Clear guidance on how to fix it

Examples:
- "Failed to load shows. Please try again."
- "Connection request failed. Check your internet."
- "Profile update failed. Please check all required fields."

VALIDATION MESSAGES:
For forms, you'll see:
- Real-time validation as you type
- Red text under fields with errors
- Green checkmarks for valid fields
- Clear instructions on what's needed

EMPTY STATES:
When you don't have any data yet:
- Friendly icon relevant to the section
- Clear message like "No tours yet"
- Helpful description
- Call-to-action button to get started
- Sometimes tips or suggestions

This makes the platform feel welcoming even when you're just starting out.

CONFIRMATION DIALOGS:
For destructive actions (like deleting):
- Modal pops up asking "Are you sure?"
- Explains what will happen
- "Cancel" and "Confirm" buttons clearly labeled
- Red color for delete buttons to signal caution
